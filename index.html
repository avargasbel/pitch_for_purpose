Program Overview

A competitive pitch event with real stakes

Pitch for Purpose is a competitive pitch event that showcases purpose-driven health & wellness ventures led by underrepresented college students — pairing them with mentors, feedback, and a shot at funding.

1

Build your concept

Develop a health & wellness product or brand idea worth pitching.

2

Get mentored

Work 1:1 and in groups with industry professionals ahead of the final round.

3

Pitch live

Present to a panel of investors and operators for prizes and funding.

Application Requirements

What to prepare

To complete your application, have the following ready — clearly labeled with your first and last name.

▶

A 2–3 minute video

Explain your interest in the program and why you should be selected. You'll paste a shareable link (YouTube, Drive, etc.) in the application.

▣

Your flyer

A flyer for your brand, clearly labeled with your first and last name. Paste a shareable link to the file.

▤

One-page concept overview

A concise summary of your idea — you can paste it directly into the application or link to a document.

Who Can Apply

  • Undergraduate or graduate students
  • Individual or teams
  • With a health & wellness consumer product or brand entrepreneurship idea

What Needs to be Submitted

  • Application form
  • 2-minute video
  • One-page concept overview
